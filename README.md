## Business Problem
Customer acquisition is only one part of sustainable business growth. While acquiring new customers is important, businesses must also understand whether those customers remain active and generate value over time.

This project performs a **SaaS/E-Commerce Cohort Retention and Customer Lifetime Value (CLTV) Analysis** to understand customer behavior after acquisition.

The analysis groups customers into cohorts based on their first purchase month and tracks their activity over subsequent months. It also calculates key financial metrics such as **Average Order Value (AOV), Purchase Frequency, and Historical Customer Lifetime Value (CLTV)**.

The project combines customer retention analysis with customer value segmentation to provide actionable insights for Product, Marketing, and Finance teams.

# 🎯 Business Problem

High-growth businesses often focus heavily on customer acquisition without sufficiently measuring whether newly acquired customers remain engaged.

A business may acquire thousands of customers but still experience poor growth if those customers churn shortly after their first purchase.

This project addresses the following business questions:

- How well are customers retained after their first purchase?
- At what stage of the customer lifecycle is churn highest?
- Which acquisition cohorts have the strongest retention?
- Which acquisition channels generate the highest-value customers?
- Which geographic segments generate the highest Customer Lifetime Value?
- How can the business improve customer retention?
- Where should marketing resources be allocated to maximize customer value?

---

# 🎯 Business Objectives

The main objectives of the project are to:

1. Clean and prepare transactional customer data.
2. Calculate each customer's acquisition/cohort month.
3. Build a monthly cohort retention matrix.
4. Calculate absolute and percentage customer retention.
5. Visualize retention using a cohort heatmap.
6. Analyze retention decay over time.
7. Calculate Average Order Value (AOV).
8. Calculate Purchase Frequency.
9. Calculate Historical Customer Lifetime Value (CLTV).
10. Segment customers by acquisition channel and geographic region.
11. Identify high-value and low-value customer segments.
12. Translate analytical findings into actionable business recommendations.

# 🔄 Project Methodology

## Week 1 — Transactional Data Cleaning & Wrangling

The first stage focused on transforming the raw transactional dataset into an analysis-ready dataset.

### Data Cleaning Steps

The following activities were performed:

- Loaded the raw dataset using Pandas.
- Inspected the dataset structure.
- Checked data types.
- Checked missing values.
- Checked duplicate records.
- Validated customer IDs.
- Converted transaction dates to datetime.
- Created transaction month.
- Calculated each customer's first transaction.
- Created the Cohort Month.
- Exported the cleaned dataset.
